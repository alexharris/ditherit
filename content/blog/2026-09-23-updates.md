Fixed a bunch of little things, bug fixes and stuff. The most noticable thing will be that the default starter images are all different now, and hopefully better! We will see.

- New default example images covering photos, transparent PNGs, and animated GIFs
- Fixed wrong-color "blobs" that could bleed out of areas your palette can't reproduce when using error diffusion
- Large images are now automatically scaled down on upload instead of being rejected, and files up to 100 MB are supported
- A loading spinner now shows while large images are being added and analyzed, so the app doesnt just seem to be hanging
- The Pixeliness option is hidden for now, I think it is just overly confusing and not very good
- Removed the performance benchmark page, which no one even knew existed. Need to make this better
