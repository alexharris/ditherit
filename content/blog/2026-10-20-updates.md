A few little tweaks:

- Settings are now automatically stored in the URL, so you can bookmark/share specific configuations
- "Copy settings" is not needed anymore since you can just grab the URL from the url bar...it is replaced with "clear all" since reloading the page no longer resets everything.
- Download and Download All now open a dialog with the available formats and file sizes instead of a small dropdown, which mostly allows me to put a little "support" prompt to encourage donations ;)
